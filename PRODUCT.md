# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Members of the public checking Argentine exchange rates. They need to identify the current official and blue dollar quotes first, compare the remaining market rates, and understand recent movement over time without interpreting operational or financial jargon.

## Product Purpose

BlueHouse presents trustworthy, current exchange-rate information and its recent history. Success means a visitor can quickly confirm data freshness, read the official and blue rates, compare alternative rates, and understand how all seven rates have moved across individual updates.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exact, utilitarian. The interface should feel dependable and current without manufacturing urgency.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a crypto trading terminal. Avoid neon market colors, speculative language, ticker-like density, alarmist movement indicators, and decorative financial imagery. Avoid generic admin-dashboard grids that give every metric equal visual weight.

## Design Principles

- Put the public's primary comparison, official versus blue, before operational detail.
- Make freshness and collection health visible but quiet.
- Use hierarchy to distinguish primary rates, secondary rates, and historical context.
- Present movement factually without implying investment advice or urgency.
- Prefer direct labels and familiar financial conventions over dashboard jargon.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve semantic reading order, keyboard access, visible focus, sufficient contrast, non-color status cues, and reduced-motion preferences. Keep financial values legible at mobile sizes and do not rely on hover to expose essential information.
